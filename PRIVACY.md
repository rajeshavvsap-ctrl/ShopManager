# Privacy Policy – Shop Manager

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

Shop Manager is a billing and stock app for small cloth and antique jewellery stores, developed by Rajesh Avvaru.

**Data we collect:** none. The app does not collect, send or share any personal data with the developer or with third parties.

**Data stored on your device:** items, prices, stock quantities, sales bills (including an optional customer name and mobile number that you type in), supplier purchases and payments, and your store name and UPI ID. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

**Barcode scanning:** the app uses the Google code scanner provided by Google Play services. Camera images are processed on the device by Google Play services and are not sent to the developer. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**UPI payments:** the app only shows a UPI QR code for the customer to scan with their own UPI app. The app does not process payments and never sees bank or card details.

**Children:** the app is meant for shop owners and is not directed at children.
